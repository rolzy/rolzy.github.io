## How I met your mum
For the uninitiated, Runescape is a 23-years-old massively multiplayer online (MMO) role-playing game. 
It is one of the poster child online-games of my generation, and the one that I spent the most hours on. 
Most players agree that the "Golden Age" of Runescape was between 2005-2007, when I first started playing the game.

Every generation has a word that acts as a key to unlock a memory lane. 
For us 90's kids, one of those nostalgic words are "Miniclip.com".
As one of the first internet-natives of the world, I have had different "homepages" at all stages of my life.
Miniclip.com is a website that hosted hundreds of browser game. It was also my first "homepage".

One day, when I was 9 years old, I noticed a game that was always high up in the Miniclip.com rankings.
Four pebbles, each with a letter to spell out RUNE.
A sword.
Five more pebbles to spell out SCAPE. 
This was when I met RuneScape.
<INSERT SCREENSHOT>

I still remember the time when I created my first RuneScape account.
There was a tickbox in the form that asked "Are you over the age of 13?"
As I hover over the blank tickbox, my 9-year-old heart was pounding with fear and thrill.
"Am I going to jail if they find out?"

Alas, I made my account, and so far I haven't been sent to jail (its past the limitation period by now, right?)
The time that follows is one of my most fond memories of my childhood. 
Walking around the world of Gileinor.
Killing a chicken. Looting its meat. Cutting down a tree. Lighting the logs. Cooking the chicken in the fire. 
The idea of having a representation of "me". In another world. That is filled with "other people".

The whole experience was extremely exciting and enlightening for 9 year-old me.

I got HOOKED. Oh boy was I hooked.
Ever since then, I've been playing Runescape on and off up to today.


- I have terminal I-need-to-be-doing-something-constructive-phobia
- A part of me is always trying to be a something, a someone, a _nanimono_
    - Nanimono is a Japanese novel by Ryo Asai...
- A indoor hobby like gaming was not helping me out to be a nanimono
- People around me would actively persuade me that gaming is a bad thing
    - Parents actively banned PC time (which I think is the worst thing you can do)
    - My grandpa kept telling horror stories of my cousin failing high school due to games
    - Friends telling me games don't "produce anything"

- But I kept playing as an adult
    - Achieved quest cape, diary cape
    - Did some raids
    - 800M bank
    - I had awareness of how much I was playing, I had a full time job, I was studying part-time, I had a wife and bought a house, my life was 
    - But I still wanted to be a nanimono. I wasn't happy with where I was
    - "What you do at 8PM is what you do in 8 years time"
    - The voices of villifying games in my head
    - Games weren't fun anymore
    - One day I had enough, dropped everything, logged out and asked Jagex to delete my account

- And I went cold turkey on games. Once and for all.
    - I got into running
    - Spent more time doing yoga and working out
    - Started doing a blog and doing ok at it
    - Taking IT certifications

- But I keep looking for a no-brainer hobby that I can do after a long days work
- Do I regret it? no not really, as long as I'm enjoying it now that is all that matters I guess
